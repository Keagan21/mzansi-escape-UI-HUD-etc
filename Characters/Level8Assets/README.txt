# Level 8 assets

You can drop GLBs in **either**:

- `collab/public/Level8Assets/` (recommended — the game loads them at `/Level8Assets/File.glb` without a rebuild import)
- this folder (for your own copies)

The level already uses:

- `shell3D.glb`, `engen3D.glb`, `KFC3D.glb` (copied to public/Level8Assets)
- Level 1 taxi GLB (parked on the curb + moving on named streets)
- Level 2 cars and playable characters
- Corridor shops as street vendors

Named streets: Vilakazi Ridge, Chris Hani Drive, Clinic Road, School Road, Spaza Lane.

## Optional files (public/Level8Assets)

House1–6.glb, Clinic.glb, School.glb, Spaza.glb, Home.glb, Vendor1.glb, Vendor2.glb, StreetVendor.glb, VendorCart.glb
